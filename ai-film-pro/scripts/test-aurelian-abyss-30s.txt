"THE AURELIAN ABYSS"
Genre: Fantasy adventure — Setting: a deep marine trench choked with towering shelf corals, glowing blue anemones, and drifting golden plankton, lit only by a massive bioluminescent creature's own golden glow

CHARACTERS
EVAN — A young marine biologist in his mid-20s. Slim, athletic build, average height. Light tan skin, wide expressive eyes. Wet, dark wavy hair floating loosely in the water, visible through a clear dive mask. Wearing a dark-grey and amber-accented composite wetsuit, a heavy-duty utility harness, a glowing wrist-mounted sonar tablet, and a compact titanium rebreather mouthpiece — no full face covering, mouth and eyes clearly visible. Voice: male_young.

STAGING NOTES (apply to every shot): a massive, serpentine, bioluminescent golden sea-creature always stays frame-left, Evan always stays frame-right — screen direction never flips. Evan's dark-grey/amber wetsuit and glowing rebreather mask stay visually identical shot to shot. Lighting is intense rim-light from the creature's own golden glow, casting sharp highlights on the canyon walls, against a palette of abyssal cobalt and electric indigo water. No music — only diegetic sound: hydrophone rumble, regulator breaths, deep-sea hums, ascending bubbles.

SHOT LIST
1. Wide tracking shot, moving left to right through a cobalt-blue coral canyon. Evan swims frame-right, his eyes tracking a massive, serpentine golden silhouette moving fluidly ahead of him at frame-left. A deep hydrophone rumble and a rhythmic mechanical inhaling sound roll through the water.
2. Over-the-shoulder shot past the golden creature's flank, looking at Evan. His eyes widen behind his mask as golden plankton drifts between them. EVAN, voice muffled and metallic through the rebreather: "You're still here..."
3. Close-up on Evan's bare right hand as he reaches it slowly forward, fingers trembling, hovering inches from the creature's warm, pulsing golden scales. EVAN, barely audible, a whispered sigh: "...they don't know..."
4. Close-up, low angle on Evan's face as his fingers make contact with the creature just off-screen. Golden light intensifies across his face, his eyes soften into pure joy, and a muffled laugh releases a small cloud of bubbles from his regulator.
5. Low-angle medium shot, camera panning up. The golden creature sweeps past, its tail gently wrapping around Evan and spinning him upward into a warm, swirling vortex of shimmering bubbles. A low-frequency purring resonates through the water.
6. High-angle extreme wide shot looking straight down. Evan floats on his back, arms outstretched, spinning joyfully inside the ascending column of golden bubbles as the creature circles him once more before fading into the dark deep below.
